# the-two-recoveries
Power BI analysis of U.S. college mental health and enrollment trends from 2019–2025


### Did enrollment and student wellbeing recover together?

A Power BI decision-support analysis examining U.S. college enrollment recovery and student mental health trends from 2019–2025.

## Key Findings

- Anxiety remained 1.9 percentage points above the 2019 baseline.
- Anxiety peaked at 35.1% in Spring 2022.
- Female students reported higher anxiety levels than male students across all analyzed periods.
- National enrollment recovered to approximately pre-pandemic levels, but recovery varied considerably across states.

## Project Overview

This project explores whether the recovery in U.S. college enrollment after the COVID-19 pandemic was accompanied by a similar recovery in student mental wellbeing.

The analysis combines enrollment, population, and student mental health data to examine trends over time, differences by gender, and geographic variation across U.S. states.
